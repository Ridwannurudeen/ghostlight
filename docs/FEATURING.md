## `#rl-mobile-featuring`

**Ghostlight**

World: https://decentraland.org/jump/?realm=ghostlight.dcl.eth

**Short description**

Decode three-emote avatar ghosts, then leave your own charade for the next player. On mobile, where there are
no DMs, voice, or scene chat, players communicate through performance.

**Gameplay**

Watch an avatar ghost perform three ordered clues—START, ACTION, and REACTION—then choose its meaning from three
large answer cards. Exactly one card is the author's assigned phrase, and the cards stay locked until the first
complete performance. The authoritative server also rejects a first guess before the 7.5-second sequence deadline,
including in a live race. Then take the stage: choose a phrase from the curated deck, select one of two validated
emotes for each beat, preview the performance on your avatar ghost, and post it for a later visitor. Signed-in
players can return to see attempts, Answer-Back replies, and waiting Ghost Mail; play the daily themed Tonight's
Show; earn participation titles; or add an Answer-Back performance that later plays as an alternating two-avatar
duet. They can also send a private Ghost Mail to a real recent performer; it stays outside the public pool and
boards and is served only when that recipient returns. A copy-invite action gives friends a general World link;
eligible content is selected by the server after arrival, so the link does not claim to target the inviter's
performance. The six-performance House fallback rotation is clearly labeled and excluded from player
statistics and boards. The interface, 30-phrase playable deck, and 120 historical phrase translations are
available in English, Spanish, and Portuguese.

**Novel mechanic for mobile players**

Time-shifted avatar charades turn three emotes into a message another mobile player can decode later, even
when the two players never overlap. Answer-Back extends that message into an asynchronous duet without text,
voice, or free-form input. Ghost Mail targets the asynchronous performance at a recent real performer without an
address field or free text.

**Mobile controls and UI safety**

Every required custom game action is in a left-side playbill panel rendered inside the SDK's interactable safe
area, with source-layout targets at least 96 px tall and no required control in the bottom-right joystick area.
Real-device recording remains the gate for physical scaling and thumb reach. Native mobile action buttons are
hidden while the movement joystick remains available; decoding and authoring require no gesture or tap on a 3D
object. No screen exposes more than five game buttons; authoring is phased by
START, ACTION, and REACTION, and keeps Post unavailable until the complete three-beat preview finishes. Starting
playback alone is not enough to unlock posting. The reaction tray replaces other actions while open. A top-right
HOW TO PLAY control opens five short localized
instructions with SETTINGS and BACK actions, and Settings links back to that guide. Persistent localized hints
identify the next action across the core flow. Settings cycles English, Spanish, and Portuguese and offers
full/quiet/off sound, reduced motion, and 20% larger text for the current visit. Players
watching an active live round can press Laugh, Gasp, or Applause; each real press triggers a local avatar emote and
stamp while the rate-limited server relays the reaction only to the other players present.

Attach a 30–60 second recording captured in the Decentraland mobile app on a real phone. Show the visible
mobile controls, a ghost performance, an answer tap and reveal, and three-emote authoring with the full preview
finishing before Post unlocks, plus the Answer-Back duet. Use only real-device gameplay, with no desktop or editor
footage. Do not describe the mobile pass as complete until that recording and the matching device-checklist run
exist for the submitted commit.
